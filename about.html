About VAWnet

VAWnet is a project of the National Resource Center on Domestic Violence — an online library of thousands of materials on domestic violence, sexual violence and related issues, built for advocates and the people who support them.

The library has been maintained since 1998 by librarians and technical assistance specialists at the National Resource Center on Domestic Violence. Every item is reviewed before it is added, tagged against the same topic framework, and dated so you can see how current it is.

Special Collections go a step further: they gather the most useful materials on a single subject, written up by a subject specialist and refreshed as the field changes. Nothing here sits behind a paywall or a login — an account only exists so you can save what you find.
